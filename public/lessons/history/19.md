## Overview

The instability of 193 CE ends with Septimius Severus as the successful claimant. Match his rivals to locations: Pescennius Niger in the east, Clodius Albinus in the west. Military support and the imperial family dominate his dynasty.

Caracalla and Geta do not sustain joint rule. Macrinus briefly interrupts Severan power before Elagabalus and Severus Alexander. Julia Domna, Julia Maesa, and Julia Mamaea are important family figures. Alexander's death in 235 CE introduces rapid military successions.

## Key connections

- Septimius Severus was born at **Leptis Magna** in North Africa. His wife was Julia Domna.

- Septimius defeated Clodius Albinus at **Lugdunum** in 197 CE. He had defeated Pescennius Niger at Issus in 194 CE.

- The brother killed on Caracalla's orders was **Geta**. Both were sons of Septimius Severus and Julia Domna.

- Caracalla's citizenship grant was the Constitutio **Antoniniana**. It extended citizenship across the Empire's free population.

- The prefect who became emperor after Caracalla was **Macrinus**. He was outside the Severan family.

- The final Severan emperor was Severus **Alexander**. His death in 235 CE begins the barracks-emperor period.
