## Overview

The war grows out of Carthaginian activity in Spain and the siege of Saguntum. Hannibal brings the conflict into Italy and wins dramatic victories. Rome responds with Fabius' delaying strategy, resistance by allied communities, and campaigns outside Italy.

Scipio's progress in Spain and Africa changes the war's direction. Keep Hasdrubal Barca, Hannibal's brother, distinct from Hasdrubal son of Gisgo. Match each battlefield to a result: Cannae is Hannibal's great victory, the Metaurus blocks reinforcement, and Zama ends the war in Rome's favor.

## Key connections

- The siege that helped trigger the Second Punic War was at **Saguntum**. Hannibal attacked the Spanish city in 219 BCE.

- Hannibal's encirclement victory of 216 BCE was at **Cannae**. The Roman commanders were Varro and Aemilius Paullus.

- Fabius Maximus earned the name **Cunctator** for delaying battle. The guide calls him the shield of Rome.

- Hasdrubal Barca died at the **Metaurus** River in 207 BCE. Claudius Nero and Livius Salinator commanded the Romans.

- Scipio's Numidian ally at Zama was **Masinissa**. His cavalry supported the Roman victory.

- Scipio defeated Hannibal at **Zama** in 202 BCE. The victory ended the Second Punic War.
